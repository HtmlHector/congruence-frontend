# Foundation

Foundation is the interface layer for this starter. Ink and paper, Geist type, hairlines and compact controls, with Notion-style information architecture around them.

The full reference lives at `/design-system` in the app. Storybook mirrors the component states and the two themes.

## Principles

1. Structure over decoration. Hierarchy comes from scale, weight, hairlines and space.
2. Ink is the brand. Near-black carries actions, links and selected states.
3. One hue rule. Color appears for status only.
4. Density with air. Compact controls, generous space between groups.
5. Motion confirms state. Nothing enters, loops or parallaxes for effect.

## Theme

Use the Theme toolbar above the canvas to switch between light and dark. Both themes read from the same `--foundation-*` tokens defined in `src/app/globals.css`.

AppShell, Sidebar, WorkspaceSwitcher, ResourceHeader, ResourceGrid, EmptyState and RealtimeProvider are headless. The stories show a Foundation composition around their `data-slot` hooks.
